I'm Sam. I work on cryptography, computational complexity, and formal verification.

I like understanding what computers can and cannot do, building useful tools, and making mathematical arguments precise enough for a machine to check.

Here you'll find privacy protocols, complexity theory in Lean, terminal tools, and experiments that occasionally turn into games.

- **Complexity Theory:** [complexitylib](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/complexitylib) and my [CSLib contributions](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3ASamuelSchlesinger).
- **Standards Drafts:** [Moderation of unLinkable Endorsements (MoLE)](https://github.com/Moderation-of-unLinkable-Endorsements).
- **Teaching:** [Leanlings](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/leanlings), small exercises for learning Lean, and [Computable Secrets](https://www.youtube.com/@computablesecrets), my YouTube channel.
