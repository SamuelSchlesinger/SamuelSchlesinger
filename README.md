I'm Samuel. I work on cryptography, computational complexity, and formal verification.

I like understanding what computers can and cannot do, building useful tools, and making mathematical arguments precise enough for a machine to check.

Here you'll find privacy protocols, complexity theory in Lean, terminal tools, and experiments that occasionally turn into games.

- **Complexity theory:** [complexitylib](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/complexitylib) and my [CSLib contributions](https://github.com/leanprover/cslib/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3ASamuelSchlesinger) — making computational complexity precise in Lean.
- **Cryptography:** [Anonymous Credit Tokens](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/anonymous-credit-tokens) — an experimental implementation of privacy-preserving credit tokens in Rust.
- **Tools:** [tshts](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/tshts), a terminal spreadsheet, and [Generalist](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/generalist), an AI agent.
- **Learning:** [Leanlings](https://github.com/SamuelSchlesinger/leanlings), small exercises for learning Lean, and [Computable Secrets](https://www.youtube.com/@computablesecrets), my YouTube channel.
